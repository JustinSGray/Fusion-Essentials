# Example: sculpted acetate eyewear with moving workholding

Use this only when the product calls for these features. It illustrates a review card for a
brief covering sculpted eyewear, lens retention, expanding collets, alternating toggle
clamps, CAM and a fitted case. It does not add those requirements to other products.

| Stage | Required result | Evidence before advancing |
|---|---|---|
| Form | Reference-informed eyewear with intentional wrap, bridge/nose transitions, changing rim sections and tapered bent temples | Matched front, side, top and three-quarter views; readable close-ups, sections and available reflection/curvature checks. Repair flat, bulky or pinched regions before detailed fixtures or CAM. A softened flat extrusion fails this particular sculpted-product requirement; sampled G1 does not establish Class-A quality. |
| Lens and hinge interfaces | Continuous retention grooves, matching lens edges, credible assembly and hinge hardware | Groove sections at bridge, brow, outer and lower rim, transition continuity and remaining material. Hinge clearances and the opening/folding sweep. Label deformation and fitting assumptions. |
| Workholding | Research-informed toggle mechanisms and actuated, slotted collets with credible contact and access | Compare with the selected mechanism references. Drive coupled members through closed, unlocked and clear states; section contacts; identify restraint retained at each transition. Posed expansion is not proof of elasticity or holding force. |
| CAM | Manufacture required grooves, surfaces and hinge provisions using matching fixture states | Inspect each state's part, stock, cutter, shank, holder and fixtures. Measure access/clearance and use supported simulation. Show retract and clamp-transition order. Keep generated motion, collision evidence and machine-control implementation distinct. Missing critical machining remains a failed criterion. |
| Delivery | Useful drawings and exports, followed by the requested fitted case | Inspect dimensions, sections, fits, materials and setup information. Size the case from actual folded glasses; check lining, clearance, insertion and closure motion. Page count is not completion. |

Choose dimensions and tolerances from the intended fit and researched process. A reviewer
must see the same required criteria as the executor. The important early decision is whether
the glasses' form and interfaces justify advancing to manufacture; an acknowledged placeholder
should not consume the rest of the run in CAM and drawing production.

A representative design-change-and-restore exercise can test editability. If the product
brief does not require it, keep it optional rather than adding a hidden grading condition.
